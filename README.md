# Speech Assist

An open-source app for iOS and Android that helps people communicate during phone calls by typing what they want to say and having it spoken aloud to the other person.

## The problem

People who stammer or otherwise find speaking difficult may know exactly what they want to say, but struggle to express it during a phone call. Speech Assist aims to make everyday calls easier and more independent, while keeping the user in control of their words and timing.

## The goal

Build a simple, reliable, accessible app that does one thing exceptionally well:

**I type, and the person on the call hears it.**

Apple Live Speech is the functional baseline. Speech Assist aims to provide that core experience as a standalone, open-source app across iOS and Android.

## MVP

- Type a message during a phone call.
- Have the message spoken clearly to the other person.
- Keep the interaction fast, simple, and dependable.
- Let the user control when speech starts and stops.
- Support iOS and Android, accounting for each platform’s call-audio capabilities and restrictions.

## Future possibilities

- Record and replay phrases in the user’s own voice.
- Support more languages and voice options.
- Adopt newer speech technology where it improves latency, quality, or accessibility.
- Offer optional transcripts or call recordings with clear consent, privacy, retention, and deletion controls.
- Add practical quality-of-life features based on feedback from people who use the app.

## Principles

- User control over every spoken message.
- Accessibility and reliability before feature count.
- A focused MVP before advanced AI features.
- Open development and community contributions.
- Honest, measured performance claims.

## Status

Planning only. This repository currently contains the project goal; there is no implementation or released app yet. Architecture, licensing, and platform-specific call integration will be decided during development.
